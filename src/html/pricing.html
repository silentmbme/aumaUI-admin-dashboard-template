Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help you choose.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan as your workspace needs change.

What happens when I choose yearly billing?

You pay for the year upfront and receive the displayed yearly rate, which is about 17% less than monthly billing.

Can I add more team members?

The Team plan includes up to 10 members. Business supports unlimited members and custom workspace needs.

Do you offer help choosing a business plan?

Use the Business plan contact action and our team can help match the plan to your requirements.
