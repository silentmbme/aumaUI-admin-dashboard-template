Project description

A secure and user-friendly self-service portal designed to simplify client onboarding, account management, and support requests. Atlas brings essential client services together in one centralized platform, making it easier for users to access information, complete required actions, and manage their accounts efficiently.

The portal provides clear account information, guided workflows, document and profile management, and streamlined support interactions.
